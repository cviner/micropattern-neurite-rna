Supplementary File 5. LayoutEditor GDSII design for the micropattern, in nanometre database units, all on layer 1.

Measured from the file: 25 µm square nodes at 125 µm centre-to-centre, joined by 5 µm channels that span the 100 µm between nodes; 127 nodes per row and 300 rows at a 50 µm pitch, 38,100 nodes over about 15.8 mm × 15.0 mm. These match the Methods and Figure 1.

The nodes and channels must be the clear areas of the mask. UV through them is what dissolves the PDMP and exposes the poly-D-lysine. The top cell also holds a text label and a 75 mm plate outline outside the pattern, and a zero-width 22 mm square marking the coverslip.
